# 60-second demo video script

**0–7 seconds | The idea**  
Show the home screen. Caption: “Some stories deserve a light that never goes out.”

**7–15 seconds | Open AI**  
Click “Load Local AI”. Show “LOCAL AI READY”. Caption: “SmolLM2 runs on this device. No AI API key. No inference server.”

**15–30 seconds | Build a memory**  
Click “Try an example”. Show the example details, then click “Weave this memory”. Show the saved remembrance card in the archive.

**30–43 seconds | Ask the Echo**  
Open “Ask the Echo”. Ask: “What food did they love?” Show the answer. Caption: “Answers are based on details saved by the user. This is an archive assistant, not a spirit.”

**43–53 seconds | Privacy and control**  
Show the archive and Backup button. Caption: “Stories and photos stay in this browser. Export your archive any time.”

**53–60 seconds | Open innovation**  
Show the open-source section. Caption: “Open weights. Browser inference. Forkable code. Private memories.”

## Recording tips

- Keep the browser zoom at 90% or 100% so the page looks balanced.
- Wait until the local model is ready before recording.
- Use the built-in example so you do not need to show a real person's private story or photograph.
- Record captions instead of speaking over the demo if the room is noisy.
